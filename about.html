A small studio with a big kiln.

Oma began in 2021 in a rented garage in Lahore. Today a team of five throws, glazes and packs every order by hand.

What we care about.

Honest shapes

We make forms that feel good to hold before we make them look good on a shelf.

Small batches

We fire about 80 pieces at a time, so we can check every one before it leaves.

Local clay

Our stoneware body is mixed in Punjab, which keeps shipping short and costs fair.

Made to last

If a handle ever snaps in normal use, we will repair or replace it.
