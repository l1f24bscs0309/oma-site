From clay to cupboard.

Each piece takes about three weeks and passes through six hands-on steps.

  1. Wedge

    We knead the clay to remove air pockets so it spins evenly on the wheel.

  2. Throw

    The clay is centered and opened on the wheel, then pulled up into its final shape.

  3. Trim

    After a day of drying, we trim the base and add handles and feet.

  4. Bisque fire

    A first firing at 980°C makes the piece hard enough to glaze.

  5. Glaze

    Each piece is dipped by hand in cobalt, celadon, ochre or plum.

  6. Glaze fire

    A second firing at 1,240°C melts the glaze into a glassy, food-safe surface.
