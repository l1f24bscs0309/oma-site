Looking after your pieces.

Stoneware is tough. A few habits will keep the glaze bright for decades.

Can I use the dishwasher?

Yes. Every glaze is dishwasher safe. Hand washing keeps the color deeper for longer.

Can I use the microwave or oven?

Yes to both. Oven use is safe up to 220°C. Avoid moving a hot piece straight onto a cold or wet surface.

What are the small marks on the base?

They are kiln stilt marks and wheel lines. They show the piece was made by hand.

What if a piece arrives damaged?

Send us a photo within 7 days through the contact page and we will replace it.

Is the glaze food safe?

Yes. All glazes are lead-free and tested for food contact.
